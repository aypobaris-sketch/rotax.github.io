Sick in Istanbul and cannot get to a pharmacy?

This is the situation we solve every day. You are a visitor or you live here, you need medicine, and one of these is true: it is the middle of the night, you do not speak Turkish, you do not know which pharmacy is open, or you simply cannot leave — because you are ill, or someone you are looking after is.

Write to us on WhatsApp in English. Send what you need and your address. We do the rest.

How Turkish pharmacies work (the part nobody explains)

Pharmacies are called "eczane" and have a red "E" sign. Most close in the evening.

At night there is always a duty pharmacy — the "nöbetçi eczane". Pharmacies take turns staying open overnight and on public holidays; the local pharmacists' chamber sets the rota. The important part: the duty pharmacy is not the closest one to you. It is whichever pharmacy's turn it is that night, which is often a long way away.

Many medicines are available without a prescription here. Painkillers, fever reducers, cold and flu remedies, throat lozenges, nasal sprays, vitamins, most stomach remedies — you can simply ask for them by name. Some medicines that need a prescription in your country do not need one in Turkey, and the reverse is also true.

Prescription medicine needs a prescription. If a Turkish doctor saw you, the prescription is usually electronic ("e-reçete") and linked to your ID — the pharmacy pulls it from the system. A photo of a paper prescription usually works too.

What we do — and what we do not

We are a courier, not a pharmacy. We do not sell medicine and we cannot. In Turkey only a licensed pharmacy may dispense it, and prescription medicine cannot legally be sold online. Any website offering to sell you prescription drugs in Turkey is not a pharmacy.

What we do is the journey. You tell us what you need; we find a pharmacy that is open and has it, buy it there, and bring it to your address. The purchase stays between you and the pharmacy — we just carry it.

How it works, step by step

1. Message us. On WhatsApp, in English. Tell us what you need and your address — a hotel name, an Airbnb address or a pin from your map app is fine.

2. We quote the courier fee. Before anything happens, so there is no surprise later.

3. We find the pharmacy. We check which pharmacies are open and which one actually has your item. You do not have to search anything.

4. The courier buys it and rides straight to you. No other stops, no sorting centre.

5. You pay on delivery. The courier already paid the pharmacy; you pay the courier in cash — medicine plus the courier fee. We hand you the pharmacy receipt so you can see exactly what was paid.

What we can bring from a pharmacy

Without a prescription: painkillers, fever reducers, cough syrup, throat lozenges, nasal spray, vitamins, rehydration salts, antiseptics, plasters and bandages.

Baby items: formula, bottles, nappies, nappy cream, baby thermometers. This is the most common night-time request we get.

Medical equipment: thermometers, blood-pressure monitors, blood-sugar test strips, syringes, masks, supports and braces.

With a prescription: antibiotics, insulin, blood-pressure and heart medication, chronic-illness medication.

Prices and payment

Pharmacy deliveries use a flat rate — no distance, night or weekend surcharge. We tell you the amount before the courier leaves.

The medicine itself is a separate cost and there is no limit on it — the courier pays the pharmacy whatever it comes to and you settle up on delivery. Payment is in cash, Turkish lira. You can see our general courier rates on the price calculator.

Where we go and how long it takes

All 39 districts of Istanbul, European and Asian side, 24 hours a day. Typically 30–45 minutes when the pharmacy is near you; longer to a distant district, and longest when the trip crosses the Bosphorus.

If you need it by a specific time, say so in your first message. If we cannot make it, we will tell you so rather than promise.

Not just medicine

We are a motorcycle courier company. We also move documents — contracts, notary papers, anything needing a signature — and small parcels anywhere in Istanbul, same day. If you left your passport at a hotel across the city or need a package moved before a flight, that is an urgent courier job and we do it.

We do not carry large or heavy items — anything that does not fit in a motorcycle case — and we do not travel outside Istanbul.

Frequently asked

Questions

Can I buy medicine online in Turkey?

No. Prescription medicine cannot legally be sold online in Turkey — only licensed pharmacies may dispense it. We are a courier, not a pharmacy: we collect the medicine from a pharmacy and bring it to you. The sale stays with the pharmacy.

Do I need a prescription?

For prescription medicine, yes. If you have a Turkish e-prescription, the courier only needs your ID number. A photo of a paper prescription is usually enough. Many common medicines — painkillers, fever reducers, cold remedies, vitamins — are sold over the counter in Turkey and need no prescription.

What is a 'nöbetçi eczane'?

It is the duty pharmacy. Turkish pharmacies take turns staying open at night and on holidays, and the local pharmacists' chamber sets the rota. The duty pharmacy is not the nearest one — it is whichever pharmacy's turn it is, so it is often in another neighbourhood. We find it for you.

How much does it cost?

We tell you the price before the courier leaves, so there is no surprise at the door. Pharmacy deliveries use a flat rate with no night or weekend surcharge. The medicine itself is a separate cost.

How do I pay?

The courier pays the pharmacy first and you pay the courier on delivery — cash. You do not need to send money in advance. There is no limit on the medicine amount, and we hand you the pharmacy receipt.

How long does it take?

Usually 30–45 minutes if the pharmacy is nearby, longer if it is in another district or across the Bosphorus. We give you a time estimate when we take the order and tell you honestly if it will not work.

Do you speak English?

Yes — write to us on WhatsApp in English. Writing is easier than calling: addresses stay in text and nothing gets lost.

Which areas do you cover?

All 39 districts of Istanbul, both the European and Asian sides, 24 hours a day, every day.

Can you bring things other than medicine?

Yes. Baby formula and nappies, thermometers, blood-pressure monitors, blood-sugar strips, plasters and other pharmacy items. We also deliver documents and small parcels anywhere in Istanbul.

Need medicine now?

Message us in English on WhatsApp with what you need and your address. We will tell you the price and the time, then go.
